As an avid SCP fan this books captures the essence of the unnatural and cryptic that defines the community.

The way that the author describes things you can not remember and then make you forget them with the characters is precise and mind blowing.

While this book is all free in the SCP website, the way that it has been written an edited gave me goosebumps. I have read the stories several times and I always get captivated by them.

My biggest complain about this book is that it ends. I wish it would never end.
