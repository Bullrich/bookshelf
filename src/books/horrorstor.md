The physical book itself is very interesting, it has a lot of images and style trying to copy an Ikea catalog.

The story is good, it starts feeling like SCP-3008 (the infinite IKEA) but then transforms more into the movie Grave Encounters.

The characters are solid, tho they can be a bit 2 dimensional, and there are 3 big loose ends that aren't properly addressed.

Still, I completely devoured this book in 3 days and I recommend it to anyone who likes Horror and ikea.
